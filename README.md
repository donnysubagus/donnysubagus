## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a product builder and project manager based in Tangerang Selatan, Indonesia.
My path runs from UI/UX and graphic design to Head of Design, and now to owning
product development end to end at Elemes Group.

I combine product thinking, senior design craft and a Computer Science degree.
That lets me define scope, shape the experience and build working prototypes
with AI coding agents instead of waiting on long delivery cycles.
