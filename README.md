## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a co-founder of **Muezatia**, a small web, app and brand studio run by two
founders. I own product direction, the business model and the path from zero to
first revenue, and I stay hands-on with the build.

I like working where product thinking, design and engineering meet: scoping an
idea into an MVP, shaping the interface and shipping it as a fast, polished
website or app.
